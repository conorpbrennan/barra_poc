# It's Just Beta, an equity factor model primer
URL: https://www.itsjustbeta.com/

## Summary
Homepage for "It's Just Beta," a work-in-progress educational resource (primer) on equity factor models. It explains how factor models separate shared market movements from idiosyncratic ones. The core premise: "most of what a stock portfolio does is driven by factors like the market, industries, or styles like value and momentum."

The primer is built around the fundamental equation **r = Xf + ε** — portfolio returns decomposed into a factor-driven component and residual (idiosyncratic) movements.

The site is structured across 17 chapters (plus a mini-example source-code page) organized into five sections:
- **Foundations** (chapters 1–4): core theory behind factor models, the factor model equation, factors and exposures, and model types.
- **Construction** (chapters 5–8): practical estimation — estimation universe and coverage universe, cross-sectional regression to estimate factor returns, factor portfolios, and risk model assembly.
- **Applications** (chapters 9–13): risk attribution, performance attribution, portfolio construction, hedging, and alpha research.
- **In Practice** (chapters 14–15): evaluating a factor model, modifying a factor model.
- **Appendix** (chapters 16–17 + 18): practical considerations, reference material, mini-example source code.

Target audience: "quantitatively comfortable" readers not intimidated by mathematical formulas. Entry points: "Start with Part 01" (chapter 1) or browse the table of contents.
