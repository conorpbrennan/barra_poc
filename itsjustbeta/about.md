# About It's Just Beta
URL: https://www.itsjustbeta.com/about/

## Summary
About page for "It's Just Beta," a work-in-progress educational resource on equity factor models aimed at quantitatively sophisticated readers. The site bridges theoretical foundations with the practical applications professionals actually encounter.

The author, **Chris**, has two decades of experience in risk management and quantitative development at major financial institutions including **Deutsche Bank**, **BlueMountain Capital Management**, and **Citadel**. He studied under Nobel laureate **Gene Fama**. His professional background includes applying factor models to large portfolio analysis, risk reporting frameworks, overnight risk processes, systematic trading strategy evaluation, and proprietary model development.

The resource is intended to serve **hedge funds and family offices** seeking guidance on effective factor model implementation, covering both foundational concepts and industry-specific details, while staying accessible to readers comfortable with quantitative methods.
